# UI Design - Dog Management

## Design Philosophy
The "Grounded Obsidian" aesthetic merged with soft, organic pet-friendly elements. It should feel premium, sleek, and highly functional.

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Color**: Deep Charcoal / Obsidian (`#1A1A1A`)
- **Accent Color**: Vibrant Amber (`#FFB300`) or Soft Teal for health status.
- **Surface**: Glassmorphism with subtle blurring (BackdropFilter).
- **Typography**: Outfit or Inter (Clean, modern sans-serif).

## Interactions & Animations
- **Micro-animations**: Subtle scaling effects on profile cards.
- **Transitions**: Slide and fade transitions for screen navigation.
- **Dynamic Elements**: Pulsing alerts for urgent vaccination reminders.

## Layout
- **Mobile First**: Optimized for one-handed use with a bottom navigation bar.
- **Bento Grids**: Used for the dashboard to display health stats, next walk time, and mood.
